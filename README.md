# EMF

An open research framework for reading markets: how macro regimes, capital flows and technology
build-outs interact, and how to reason about them with explicit, testable rules.

**Educational and research use only.** Nothing here is financial, investment, legal or tax advice, a
recommendation, or an offer to buy or sell anything. There are no performance claims. The author may hold
positions in assets that come up in examples. Do your own work, and treat every rule here as a hypothesis
until you have tested it yourself.

## What is here

- `docs/`: the framework, one idea per document, written so a newcomer can follow the reasoning.
- Reference code and tests, as the framework's rules are turned into something you can run.

Each document says what is established, what is a hypothesis, and what has not been tested. Parts drafted
with an AI model's help are labeled as such; an idea is only as good as the tests it has survived.
